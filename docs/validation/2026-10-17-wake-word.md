# Wake word and long instructions on the Pixel

17 October 2026 · FR-13 (local wake word), first device evidence

## Setup

- Phone: Pixel 9 Pro (Android 17), Chintu 0.4.2 plus the `feature/wake-word` changes, signed with the release key.
- Computer: cobra, Ubuntu 26.04, Chintu Desktop 0.4.2, gateway on 127.0.0.1:8766 behind Tailscale Serve, `claude-agent` provider, project `home`, profile `develop`.
- Speech came from the Mac's speaker next to the phone (`say`: voice Rishi for "Chintu", Daniel or Samantha for instructions), so every run is repeatable. Results were read from the phone (`adb logcat`, screenshots, audio-record history) and from cobra's gateway log (`instruction_words`, task outcome) and file system.

## Wake word engine

Vosk `vosk-model-small-en-in-0.4` (Apache-2.0, SHA-256 pinned in `apps/android/tools/android.py`), on the phone only. "chintu" is not in the model vocabulary, so the grammar spells it with in-vocabulary pieces ("chin to", "chin two", ...) plus decoys ("pin to", "into", "chicken", "stew", ...); a match is "chin"/"chen" followed by to, too, two or into (`WakePhrase`).

Offline comparison on 30 synthetic clips (5 macOS voices, 3 wake phrases and 3 decoys each):

| Model and grammar | Wake caught | False alarms |
| --- | --- | --- |
| small en-us, wake phrases only | 11/15 | 0/15 |
| small en-in, wake phrases only | 15/15 | 10/15 |
| small en-in, with decoys (shipped rule) | 14/15 | 0/15 |

Confidence thresholds only lost wake phrases and are not used.

| W3 | Phone locked: "Chintu", then a question | Pass after fix 1: screen woke, phone stayed locked, Chintu listened over the lock screen, 10 words sent, answered |

| W5 | Ordinary speech, 9 phrases x 2 voices incl. "Pintu", "chicken stew", "into", "Toronto" | 0 false wakes except the literal phrase "chin two" (1 of 2 voices) |

Microphone hand-over was checked in the audio-record history: the wake listener stops before the recogniser records and resumes after, with no client silenced.

## Fixes made during the run

1. Opening the app from the lock screen or background reconnects to the computer first; the listen request was deferred to the hands-free retry, which runs only with hands-free on, so the instruction was lost. The wake path now waits for the connection. The screen also stays on during a spoken turn, since the lock screen's timeout cut replies short.
2. The Pixel's on-device recogniser ends a session at a ~1 s pause and ignores `EXTRA_SPEECH_INPUT_COMPLETE_SILENCE_LENGTH_MILLIS`, the minimum length and plain segmented sessions; restarting it mid-sentence returned empty results. On Android 13+ Chintu now records the microphone itself (`MicStream`) and streams it into a segmented session tied to that audio source (`EXTRA_AUDIO_SOURCE`); the session ends when Chintu detects 2 s of quiet.

## Limits and open points

- The literal phrase "chin two"/"chin to" can wake Chintu.
- After an app update or a reboot the wake listener starts again only when Chintu is opened once; Android does not let it start the microphone from the background.
- No barge-in: saying "Chintu" while it speaks does nothing.
- Instructions are sent about 2 s after the speaker stops.
- The real owner's voice, battery use over a day and Bluetooth-headset capture with streaming are not measured yet.
- APK size grows from 5 MB to about 61 MB (model and native libraries).
